# MIL course catalog

Official titles, hours, and prerequisites from the **2026–2027** undergraduate catalog. Department-site titles in parentheses are legacy. Sequence required unless PMS authorizes otherwise.

## Basic (MS I–II)

| Nbr | Catalog title | Hrs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 101 | Introduction to the Army | 2 | Dept: Mountaineering & Leadership. Army LRM / what ROTC is. Offered recent springs and falls. Recruiting Prezi: time management class and rappelling; still 2 hours. |
| 102 | Foundations of Army Leadership | 2 | Dept: Developmental Skills. Life skills, first aid, survival, group interaction. Spring. |
| 201 | Army Leadership and Ethics | 3 | Prereq 101+102; PMS permission may be required. Dept: Basic Leadership. Fall. |
| 202 | Team Building and Army Doctrine | 3 | Prereq 101+102+201; PMS permission may be required. Spring. |
| 209 | *(not in course list)* | 1? | Major page: Introduction to Military Life, paired with 210. **Gap.** |
| 210 | Military Science Practicum (Leader’s Training Course) | 6 | In lieu of 101–202. Fort Knox, summer. |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 302 | Applied Tactical Army Leadership | 4 | PMS permission. Repeatable max 4. Small-unit tactics, comms, TLP. Spring. |
| 401 | Professional Leadership Skills | 3 | PMS permission. Counseling, org leadership, delegation. Fall. Dept PDF: `/rotc/documents/401.pdf`. |
| 402 | Role of the Army Officer | 4 | PMS permission. Ops, logistics, admin, justice, commissioning prep. Spring. |
| 410 | Military Science Practicum (LDAC) | 6 | PMS permission. [[advanced-camp]]. Summer. Major table also says 3–6. |
| 490 | Military Leadership Seminar | 1–3 | Repeatable to 6. PMS permission. Transition / PT / installation field time. Recent terms: none. Dept lists 1 hour. |

These PRT courses cite FM 7-22. “Recent Term(s) Offered: None” means they may be on the books without recent Banner sections. Do not assume cadets register for them every term.
