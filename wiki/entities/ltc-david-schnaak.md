# Lieutenant Colonel David Schnaak

Professor of Military Science and department chair, [[department-of-military-science-and-leadership|WKU Army ROTC]] / Hilltopper Battalion.

This is a **standing override**. The 2026–2027 undergraduate catalog still lists [[ltc-anthony-struzik]]. Do not use Struzik as current chair.

## Evidence

- Human correction filed 2026-09-08: Schnaak is PMS and chair.
- WKU News, 7 May 2026: “Lieutenant Colonel David Schnaak, Professor of Military Science,” administered the oath for 14 spring/summer 2026 commissions.
- *College Heights Herald*, 11 Nov 2025: “Department Chair Army Lt. Col. David Schnaak” at the Guthrie Tower Veterans Day ceremony.

Third-party LinkedIn (not ingested as a raw file) lists PMS at WKU from July 2025. Treat month/year as unconfirmed until a WKU source is captured.

## Role

The catalog defines the PMS as the career Army lieutenant colonel who provides academic instruction and supervision and chairs the department. PMS permission is required for advanced MIL courses and for some basic-course sequencing exceptions. PMS recommendation is an admission requirement for the [[military-leadership-bs-733|major]]. Interviews with the PMS are scheduled through [[emma-brittenham]].
