# LT Eric D. Yates

WKU Army ROTC alumnus memorialized by the battalion’s annual 5K. Facts below are from [[brandon-smith]]’s recruiting Prezi (updated 11 September 2026), not a separate obituary capture.

A 2008 WKU graduate. Killed in action 17 September 2010 after an improvised explosive device detonated near his patrol in Afghanistan. Served in the Army’s 1st Battalion, 502nd Infantry Regiment, 2nd Brigade Combat Team, 101st Airborne Division from Fort Campbell.
