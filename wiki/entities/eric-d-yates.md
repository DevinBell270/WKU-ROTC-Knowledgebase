# 1LT Eric D. Yates

Honored by the [[hilltopper-battalion]] as **1LT Eric D. Yates**, killed in action **September 17, 2010**. The battalion held the **16th annual Eric Yates memorial run** on **September 19, 2026**.

[[brandon-smith]]’s recruiting Prezi (updated 11 September 2026) adds: a **2008** WKU graduate; killed in action after an improvised explosive device detonated near his patrol in **Afghanistan**; served in the Army’s **1st Battalion, 502nd Infantry Regiment, 2nd Brigade Combat Team, 101st Airborne Division** from Fort Campbell. Prezi labels him **LT** Eric D. Yates, not 1LT.
