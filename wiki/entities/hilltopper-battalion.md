# Hilltopper Battalion

Informal / unit name for the WKU Army ROTC cadet battalion, hosted by the [[department-of-military-science-and-leadership]]. PMS: [[ltc-david-schnaak]]. Public social accounts used in captures: Instagram `@wkuarmyrotc`, Facebook `WKUROTC`.

Spring/summer 2026 commissioning class: 14 second lieutenants (active, Guard, and Reserve). See raw commencement capture for names and first assignments.

## Recruiting Prezi snapshot (updated 11 September 2026)

[[brandon-smith]]’s deck, not a roster. Quote: “Our 100+ Cadet Battalion represents over 40 different majors, including 16 who earned a 4.0 cumulative GPA last semester.” Also: “For over 100 years we continue to develop the strongest leaders preparing them to become 2nd Lieutenants in the United States Army.” Prezi tagline block includes “Est. 1918” (department history narrative uses 1919 — [[source-conflicts]]).

Training as marketed on that deck:

- PT program (whole-body; physically and mentally strong)
- [[leadership-lab]] — once a week, 2 hours
- [[field-training-exercises]] — Fort Knox, 2–3 days; same location as Advance Camp
- [[century-park]] — Track and Field Complex; named for the 100th commissioning class
- [[rotc-clubs-and-events]] — Ranger Challenge, CRT, Color Guard, [[gafpb]], [[eric-d-yates|LT Yates 5K]], Push-Up Board

Cadet spotlights on that deck: [[cdt-humble]], [[celia-johnston]], [[cadet-will]], [[kierigan-mcevoy]] (Taiwan / Flagship mention), [[dakota-hunt]] (Nurse Summer Training feature).

Cadet quotes on the department homepage (May 2024 commissions) treat the battalion as a family/unit culture, not just a set of classes. First-year cadets may live in the ROTC [[living-learning-community]]. Cadet chain of command is linked from older site nav but has not been ingested.
