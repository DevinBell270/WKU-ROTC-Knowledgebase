# Hilltopper Battalion

Informal / unit name for the WKU Army ROTC cadet battalion, hosted by the [[department-of-military-science-and-leadership]]. PMS: [[ltc-david-schnaak]]. Public social accounts used in captures: Instagram `@wkuarmyrotc`, Facebook `WKUROTC`.

Spring/summer 2026 commissioning class: 14 second lieutenants (active, Guard, and Reserve). See raw commencement capture for names and first assignments.

Cadet quotes on the department homepage (May 2024 commissions) treat the battalion as a family/unit culture, not just a set of classes. First-year cadets may live in the ROTC [[living-learning-community]]. Cadet chain of command is linked from older site nav but has not been ingested.

- **16th annual Eric Yates memorial run**, September 19, 2026, honoring [[eric-d-yates|1LT Eric D. Yates]] (KIA September 17, 2010). Captions: [[wku-rotc-social-2026-09-21]].
