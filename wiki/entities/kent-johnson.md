# Kent Johnson

Director of WKU Military Student Services, as named in WKU News 27 March 2026. Not ROTC cadre.

That article quotes him on the Top Ten 2026–2027 Military Friendly® and Military Spouse Friendly School rankings and on student involvement, including the student organization **Military Connected Students of WKU**.

The 11 November 2025 *College Heights Herald* Veterans Day story identified him as a USMC veteran and director of Military Student Services (third-party corroboration of the title; USMC affiliation is Herald-only in this wiki).

No phone, email, or office is in these captures. The WKU News article points military-affiliated students to **wku.edu/veterans**.
