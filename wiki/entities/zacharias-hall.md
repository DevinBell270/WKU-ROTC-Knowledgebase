# Zacharias Hall

WKU suite-style residence hall in the First Year Village (south campus). Built 1992, three stories, **206** students, co-ed. Two rooms share a Jack and Jill bathroom.

Housing’s hall page: available to **first-year students in a Living Learning Community**. The 2026–2027 halls index says Zacharias houses an LLC that year. The [[living-learning-community|ROTC LLC]] page currently names Zacharias as its building (subject to change).

Address: 1755 Normal Street, Bowling Green, KY 42101-6433. Front desk (270) 936-0219. Closes for Thanksgiving, winter, and spring breaks.

The page’s HTML meta description still says “upperclass students.” Visible body copy does not. Use the body.

Rates are not on the hall page. See wku.edu/housing/halls/rates.

Sister suite-style LLC hall: Meredith Hall (1775 Normal Street). Fall 2025 ROTC LLC was in Meredith; see [[living-learning-community]].
