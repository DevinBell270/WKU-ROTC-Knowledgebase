# Military Science, Minor (420)

**Hours:** 26–30, depending on advanced placement.
**Who:** students who complete the [[advanced-course|advanced course]].

## Requirements (catalog 2026–2027)

Select one basic-course path (6–10 hours):

- MIL 101, 102, 201, and 202, **or**
- MIL 210, **or**
- Credit for the basic course through military service

Then all of:

| Course | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| MIL 301 | 3 |
| MIL 302 | 4 |
| MIL 401 | 3 |
| MIL 402 | 4 |
| MIL 410 | 6 |

Department minor page matches this structure and notes hours vary with PMS-awarded advanced placement. Unlike the [[military-leadership-bs-733|major]], the minor as published does **not** offer LEAD 400 in place of MIL 410.
