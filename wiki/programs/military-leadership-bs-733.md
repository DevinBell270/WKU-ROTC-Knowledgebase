# Military Leadership, Bachelor of Science (733)

**Coordinator (catalog):** [[emma-brittenham]]
**Hours:** 36–42. Baccalaureate minimum 120 unduplicated hours. **A second major or minor is required.**
**College:** Education and Behavioral Sciences.

The major builds leaders through interdisciplinary coursework, internships, Army training and schools, community involvement, and leadership opportunities. It spans up to eight semesters. Commissioning is **optional**. Veterans and NCOs may be admitted without seeking a commission. Catalog also notes a possible organizational leadership certificate with additional requirements.

MIL courses must be taken in sequential order unless the [[ltc-david-schnaak|PMS]] authorizes otherwise.

## Admission — commission seeking (catalog)

- U.S. citizen or DA-approved alien category (approval before enrollment)
- At least 17 at enrollment; not reach 30 at commissioning (may be waived) — **age rule is contested; see [[source-conflicts]] and [[veterans-and-prior-service]]**
- Medically qualified per DA
- Minimum overall GPA 2.0
- Recommended by the PMS
- Written agreement to complete the two-year advanced course, attend Advanced Camp, accept a commission in the Army Reserve, and serve a prescribed tour

Upon completion, the student is eligible to receive a commission as a Second Lieutenant.

## Admission — prior service / NCO, not seeking a commission (catalog)

Citizenship, medical qualification, GPA 2.0, PMS recommendation. No commissioning contract.

Department site adds: non-military students seeking leadership knowledge may also be admitted; priority to commission-seeking students; admission is limited.
