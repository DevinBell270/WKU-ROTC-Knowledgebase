# Source summary: Housing LLC pages

WKU Housing & Residence Life is the operational source for [[living-learning-communities]] and the [[living-learning-community|ROTC LLC]] (location, application, linked courses, cost, waitlist, M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan). Department homepage LLC copy is recruiting/purpose language only and matches Housing’s purpose paragraph.

**Use for:** who the ROTC LLC is for, Zacharias vs prior-year Meredith, MIL 101/102 as linked courses, one-LLC rule, $75 housing fee, llc@wku.edu.

**Do not use for:** current scholarship/contract rules, commissioning, or Colonnade credit (Housing’s “major and general education” heading on MIL 101/102 is unverified against the catalog). Printed February 2026 LLC decision dates on the 2026-09-17 capture are the last cycle, not live deadlines.
