# Source summary: JROTC not required (2026-09-24)

Program-staff statement from the 2026-09-24 meeting, relayed by Devin Bell: Junior ROTC (JROTC) in high school is **not required** to join Army ROTC at WKU. Students often ask this; the answer is no.

Highest-authority operational fact for that entry question. Does **not** say JROTC earns credit or advanced placement. No ingested source says that. Published placement remains prior military service or Guard/Reserve membership only ([[veterans-and-prior-service]], catalog, department veteran page).
