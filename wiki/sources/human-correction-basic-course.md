# Source summary: Basic Course four-class correction (2026-09-24)

Program-staff statement from the 2026-09-24 meeting, relayed by Devin Bell: students may take the full two-year [[basic-course]] — four classes, MIL 101, MIL 102, MIL 201, and MIL 202 (freshman and sophomore years) — before deciding whether to contract. Recruiting copy that says students get only two classes before they have to commit is wrong.

Highest-authority operational fact for the length of the no-obligation window. Catalog 2026–2027 already matches (four-semester basic course; no obligation by participating in it). Does not erase the separate, source-backed rule that obligation begins with a written [[advanced-course]] contract, or with a [[scholarships|scholarship]] that requires agreeing to commission (a scholarship student may contract earlier).
