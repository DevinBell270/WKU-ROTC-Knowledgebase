# What is the ROTC LLC?

WKU’s **Army ROTC Living Learning Community (LLC)** is first-year housing for students enrolling in ROTC and a Military Science course. They live together (currently listed in [[zacharias-hall]]), take [[mil-catalog|MIL 101]] in the fall and MIL 102 in the spring, and work with an LLC Fellow. It is not a contract.

Campus LLCs generally: themed floors, no extra LLC fee (you pay that hall’s rate), one LLC per student, Housing Portal application after the $75 housing application, waitlists, required M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan for first-year LLC students. See [[living-learning-communities]] and [[living-learning-community]].

**Contested / caution:** building is *subject to change* (Fall 2025 ROTC was in Meredith). Do not treat MIL 101/102 as Colonnade because Housing’s LLC page says “general education.” Quote llc@wku.edu for applications; [[brandon-smith]] for ROTC enrollment.
