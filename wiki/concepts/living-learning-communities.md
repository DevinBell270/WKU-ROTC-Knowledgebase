# Living Learning Communities (WKU)

WKU Housing & Residence Life runs **Living Learning Communities (LLCs)**. Official acronym is **LLC**, not LCC.

An LLC puts students with similar academic interests on the same residence-hall floor with activities tied to those majors or interests. Housing’s stated aims: social connection, academic success, contact with faculty/staff/administrators, and an early campus support system.

The Army ROTC community is one LLC on the 2026–2027 list. Details: [[living-learning-community]].

## What you get (Housing copy)

- **No extra LLC fee.** You pay the housing rate for the hall that hosts your LLC. Some LLCs require specific classes (those may have course fees).
- Academic LLCs may include **up to two linked courses** that Housing says can count toward the major and/or General Education. Check the individual LLC page and the catalog for what actually counts.
- **Priority housing assignment** relative to non-LLC students, and **priority registration for LLC courses**.
- An **LLC Fellow** (faculty/staff) and a **Learning Contract** after you accept.

Housing cites a 2025 LLC evaluation (PDF not captured): LLC participants graduate at an 8.0% higher rate than non-LLC peers; first-generation LLC students 36.8% vs 19.0% for non-LLC first-gen; low-income LLC students +11.4 percentage points on four-year graduation. Treat as Housing’s published figures, not independently audited here.

Options and locations are **subject to change**.

First-year LLC halls named on the 2026–2027 residence-hall index: [[zacharias-hall|Zacharias]], Meredith, and Minton. Southwest Hall is described as Honors (sophomore+) and **Second-Year LLC**.

## How to apply

You may apply to **one** LLC.

1. After admission, submit the WKU **housing application** in the Admissions Application Status Portal (`apply.wku.edu/apply/status`). That application includes a $75 fee and living-preference ranking (LLC is one preference).
2. After processing (Housing: typically within 24 hours), email instructions point you to the **Housing Portal** via my.wku.edu.
3. Complete the **LLC application** and roommate profile there (Apply for Housing → academic term).

Changing LLC after submit: email **llc@wku.edu** with WKU ID. Picking a different LLC in a housing drop-down does **not** update the LLC application. LLC Fellows review applications. Space is limited; offers are not guaranteed; waitlists exist.

**Decision dates** printed on the 2026-09-17 capture (February 4 / 13 / 27 **2026**) are the 2026–2027 cycle and were already elapsed at capture. Do not quote them as upcoming. Ask Housing for the next cycle.

Accepting a placement requires confirming the Learning Contract in the Housing Portal and assigns you to that LLC’s building/floor. Waitlist does **not** block a regular housing assignment (date-received order); an LLC opening later can retarget the assignment.

## Roommate and involvement rules

LLC students must room with another student **in the same LLC**. Honors College students may choose the Honors LLC **or** a major-specific LLC, and then live in that LLC’s hall.

Expect about **1–2 hours** in a typical week for LLC activity; the Learning Contract states required attendance. First-year LLC students **must** attend **M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan**.

If you already have credit for a required LLC course, email llc@wku.edu; admission is at the Fellow’s discretion.

## After the first year

Housing offers a **Second-Year LLC**: interdisciplinary, all majors, for students who were in an LLC as first-years. Halls index: Southwest Hall.
