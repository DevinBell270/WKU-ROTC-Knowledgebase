# German Armed Forces Proficiency Badge (GAFPB)

Listed under [[rotc-clubs-and-events]] in [[brandon-smith]]’s recruiting Prezi (updated 11 September 2026).

Prezi: “The GAFPB - It’s one of only a few foreign awards that US Soldiers can wear.” To earn it, “you must complete 6 different challenges which include: Pistol Qualification, First Aid Course, Fitness Test, NBC MOPP Test, Foot March, 100 meter swim in military uniform.” Three award levels: Gold, Silver, or Bronze.

No ingested Army regulation, local SOP, or eligibility rule beyond that slide.
