# Army ROTC Living Learning Community

First-year housing community for students **enrolling in Army ROTC and a Military Science course**. They live on the same hall, take linked MS I classes, work with an LLC Fellow, and join co-curricular programming. Official name/acronym: **Living Learning Community (LLC)** — not LCC.

Campus-wide LLC rules (one LLC, application, cost, waitlist, M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan): [[living-learning-communities]].

This is **housing plus first-year MIL enrollment**. It is not a contract, scholarship, or commissioning obligation. [[basic-course|Basic course]] still creates no military obligation.

## Eligibility (Housing ROTC page)

- Enrolling in the ROTC program and a Military Science course
- Want to live with other Army ROTC students
- Want extracurriculars that connect classroom and out-of-class learning

Stated goals (same copy on the department homepage and the Housing ROTC LLC page): camaraderie and team building, academic persistence, faculty/staff mentorship.

## Linked courses

| Term | Course | Hours | Catalog title |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fall | [[mil-catalog\|MIL 101]] | 2 | Introduction to the Army |
| Spring | MIL 102 | 2 | Foundations of Army Leadership |

Housing also says ROTC LLC students “enroll in up to two courses each semester.” The only courses listed on the ROTC LLC page are **one** MIL course per semester. Do not invent a second linked course.

Those hours count toward residence [[basic-course]] credit and toward [[military-leadership-bs-733]] / [[military-science-minor-420]] if the student is on those programs. Housing’s heading “Satisfies major **and general education** requirements” is **not** backed by the catalog 4-year plan, which lists MIL 101/102 as major courses separate from Colonnade. Do not tell students MIL 101/102 are Colonnade/gen-ed without a catalog Colonnade listing. See [[source-conflicts]].

If the student already has credit for the required LLC course, Housing: email llc@wku.edu; the LLC Fellow decides admission.

## Location

**Current Housing ROTC LLC page (modified 2026-07-22):** [[zacharias-hall|Zacharias Hall]], flagged *subject to change*. Suite-style (two rooms share a bathroom), First Year Village, first-year LLC population, 206 beds in the building (not an ROTC-floor capacity).

**Fall 2025 assignments page:** ROTC LLC was in **Meredith Hall**, not Zacharias.

Halls index for 2026–2027: both Meredith and Zacharias house LLCs. Always use the live ROTC LLC page for the year’s building; locations move.

## How this relates to ROTC

First-year [[hilltopper-battalion]] cadets who want the community apply through Housing, not through cadre. Scholarship room/meal extras on [[scholarships]] are a separate, GPA-gated incentive. No ingested source explains how LLC placement and scholarship housing extras interact.

Apply via the Housing LLC process on [[living-learning-communities]]. Housing contact: llc@wku.edu / (270) 745-3577. ROTC questions: [[brandon-smith]] / [[contact-and-location]].
