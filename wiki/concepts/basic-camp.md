# Basic Camp (Leader’s Training Course / MIL 210)

Summer course at **Fort Knox, Kentucky** taken **in lieu of** MIL 101, 102, 201, and 202. Catalog title: Military Science Practicum (Leader’s Training Course), **6 hours**.

Purpose: compress the [[basic-course]] for students who did not take MS I–II on campus (transfers from non-ROTC schools, community-college graduates, graduate students starting a two-year program, etc.). Prior-service students receive basic-course credit and are **not** required to attend.

Students must meet Army academic and physical standards before enrollment. Catalog: to receive credit at no cost, see the PMS. Department site: register before departure.

**Contested:** length (28 days vs five weeks) and pay (~$800 vs ~$600). See [[source-conflicts]].

Cadets at camp may compete for a two-year [[scholarships|scholarship]].
