# Advanced Course

MS III–IV sequence that commissions officers for Active and Reserve components. At WKU: MIL 301, 302, 401, 402 plus a summer camp ([[advanced-camp]], credit MIL 410). A military history course, normally junior or senior year, is required before the student is fully qualified to commission.

## Published eligibility (catalog)

1. U.S. citizen or DA-approved alien category
2. ≥17 at enrollment; not reach 30 at commissioning (may be waived) — **see [[source-conflicts]]**
3. Medically qualified
4. Basic course, Basic Camp, or equivalent (including honorable-discharge veterans)
5. ≥2.0 GPA and ≥60 credit hours
6. Selected by the [[ltc-david-schnaak|PMS]]
7. Written agreement: finish the two-year advanced course, attend Advanced Camp, accept a Reserve commission, serve a prescribed active or reserve tour

PMS permission is required to enroll in 300/400-level MIL courses.

Contracted advanced-course cadets may compete for Army schools (Airborne, Air Assault, Northern Warfare) and time with active units. Voluntary; high standards.

Department site: advanced courses may be taken without seeking a commission if majoring in Military Science / Military Leadership.
