# Leadership Lab

Hands-on ROTC training as described in [[brandon-smith]]’s recruiting Prezi (updated 11 September 2026): “Once a week for 2 hours we practice what we're learning in the classroom. We prepare for Advance Camp, which is after the MSL 3 year. Ultimately we prepare to commission as an Army Officer.”

## Source notes

The department courses page describes **class** contact hours (freshman: two hours/week; sophomore: three hours/week), not this lab slide. Do not merge those figures into one “hours per week” number. Catalog [[mil-catalog]] titles and hours remain official for coursework. “Advance Camp” here is Prezi spelling for [[advanced-camp]].
