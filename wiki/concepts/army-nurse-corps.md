# Army Nurse Corps pathway

WKU ROTC + School of Nursing: military science plus Nurse Summer Training Program (NSTP), then a commission in the Army Nurse Corps if prerequisites are met.

**NSTP:** paid; normally the summer after first medical-surgical clinical or junior year; Army hospital; Army Nurse preceptor; follows preceptor schedule.

Post-commission specialty and graduate-education lists are marketing, not a guarantee of a seat.

May 2026 example: CDT Zachary Owens commissioned as an Army Nursing Corps officer on Active Duty.

No nursing-GPA, BSN, or NCLEX rules were on the captured page. Gap.
