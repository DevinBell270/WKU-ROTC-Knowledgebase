# Commissioning

Successful completion of the [[advanced-course]] at WKU earns a commission as a **Second Lieutenant** in the United States Army (Active, Reserve, or National Guard). The [[ltc-david-schnaak|PMS]] administers the oath (documented May 2026).

## Gates in the ingested sources

- Complete advanced MIL sequence (301, 302, 401, 402)
- Graduate [[advanced-camp|Advanced Camp]]
- Complete a military history course (catalog major: HIST 299 or HIST 494)
- Meet medical, citizenship, age, GPA, and PMS-selection rules
- Execute the commissioning contract (advanced-course agreement)

The [[military-leadership-bs-733|major]] alone does **not** require a commission.

Age and medical rules are high-stakes and **contested** across catalog vs veteran page. Route edge cases to [[brandon-smith]].

Spring/summer 2026: 14 lieutenants; branches included Logistics, Field Artillery, Infantry, Military Intelligence, Signal, Army Nurse Corps, Medical Services. List is in `raw/news/2026-05-07-commencement.md`.
