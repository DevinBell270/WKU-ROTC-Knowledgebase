# Basic Course

Four-semester block, normally MS I–II (freshman/sophomore). Emphasis: team and leadership development, hands-on equipment, land navigation, leadership skills. **No military obligation.**

## Residence credit at WKU

Complete MIL 101, 102, 201, and 202. Official titles: [[mil-catalog]]. The ROTC [[living-learning-community]] links MIL 101 (fall) and MIL 102 (spring) for first-year LLC students.

Department site: two contact hours/week as a freshman, three as a sophomore.

## Equivalents

- [[basic-camp|MIL 210]] at Fort Knox (catalog: 28-day Basic Course / Leader’s Training Course)
- MIL 209 + MIL 210 (major page only; MIL 209 not in course list)
- Prior military service or Guard/Reserve membership — partial or full advanced placement, depending on amount and character of service

Whatever the path, basic course **or equivalent** is required to enroll in the [[advanced-course]].
