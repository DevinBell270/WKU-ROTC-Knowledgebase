# History of WKU ROTC

Narrative from the department history page (ends **2005**). Herald 2025 repeats the 1917–1919 origin.

- **1916–19:** National Defense Act → SATC in WWI → permanent ROTC 28 January 1919. First PMST: LTC Wilford Twyman (March 1919).
- **Junior division** only at first; rifle team national champions 1928–1933.
- **1935:** Senior Infantry branch program. **1937:** Pershing Rifles B-3; first senior commissioning class.
- **1947–57:** Air Force ROTC on campus (~300 officers), then closed.
- **1954:** General Military Science (any branch). Scabbard and Blade C-11.
- **1962:** Military Science minor approved.
- **1964:** ROTC Revitalization Act (two-year program / Basic Camp). Rebelettes formed; national drill titles 1967, 1973, 1975.
- **1973–79:** Women in ROTC; first WKU female officer 2LT Lynne F. Berry (MI, 14 May 1977). Early Commissioning Program; [[simultaneous-membership-program|SMP]].
- **1980–82:** WKU cadre stood up University of Louisville ROTC.
- **1983:** Pershing Rifles / Rebelettes retired; Red Knights created.
- **1996:** Alumni Association and Hall of Fame (first 7).
- **2003–04:** Guthrie Tower Veterans Day ceremony; Murray State partnership / contracting.
- **2005:** CPT Charles F. Thomas IV Room of Honor (MS100 classroom).
