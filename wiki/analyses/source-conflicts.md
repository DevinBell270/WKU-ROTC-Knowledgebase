## Course titles

Catalog 2026–2027 uses current Cadet Command-style titles (e.g. MIL 101 *Introduction to the Army*). The department courses/major pages still use older titles (e.g. MIL 101 *Mountaineering & Leadership* / *Military Mountaineer/Leadership*).

**Handling:** [[mil-catalog]] uses catalog titles as official. Department titles are noted as legacy marketing copy.

## MIL 209

BS 733 catalog and department major page list **MIL 209** (department: *Introduction to Military Life*, 1 hour) paired with MIL 210 as a basic-course option. MIL 209 does **not** appear in the catalog MIL course list.

**Handling:** Treat as a real local requirement/option that is missing from the course inventory. Flag as a catalog gap. Do not invent a description beyond the department one-liner.

## Advanced Camp length and MIL 410 hours

Catalog department page: 33-day summer course. MIL 410 description: five-week field course, 6 hours. BS 733 table: MIL 410 **3–6** hours *or* LEAD 400. Department major: MIL 410 6 hours *or* LEAD 400 3 hours. Veteran page: six weeks.

**Handling:** For commissioning, Advanced Camp completion is required. For the major, catalog allows MIL 410 **or** LEAD 400. Hour value of MIL 410 is 6 in the course inventory and 3–6 on the major page — contested.

## Scholarship extras

Catalog: $420/month; four-year awards *may* get university room and board (budget dependent); two-/three-year *may* get room. Department scholarships page: books $1,200/year; room + 10 meals/week if GPA ≥ 3.0 and attendance at class/lab/PT; “over $80,000” total.

**Handling:** Tuition/fees + $420 stipend are the common core. Room/meal/book figures come from the department page and are budget- and GPA-dependent.
