### Hilltopper Battalion
- For over 100 years we continue to develop the strongest leaders preparing them to become 2nd Lieutenants in the United States Army.

### Physical Fitness Training
- PT! Good for you! Good for me!
- Our Cadets enjoy our PT program as it works the whole body by helping them be physically and mentally strong.

### Leadership Lab
- Hands-on portion of ROTC. Once a week for 2 hours we practice what we're learning in the classroom. We prepare for Advance Camp, which is after the MSL 3 year. Ultimately we prepare to commission as an Army Officer.

### FTX / Field Training Exercises
- We conduct our FTX's at Fort Knox, KY. This allows are Cadets to train at the location of Advance Camp. This 2-3 day training exercise provides our Cadets with some great hands-on leadership experience.

### MIL 101
- This is where it all starts! Enroll in MIL 101 to get started in the ROTC program. It's a 2 credit hour class that focuses on basic leadership skills. It also includes a time management class and rappelling!

### Chinese Flagship Program
- The Chinese Language Flagship is a Department of War initiative designed to graduate a new generation of Global Professionals who possess professional-level proficiency in Chinese which is vital to U.S. national security.
- Students can study any degree path as you take 1 additional Chinese class per semester.
- The Language Flagship program provides full funding for Contracted ROTC Cadets to participate in Flagship approved summer programs and the Capstone year abroad.
- ROTC Cadets may be awarded $250 per credit hour for Chinese language classes (if funding available).
- Learn More Here: WKU Chinese Flagship

### Living Learning Community
- The Army ROTC LLC consists of a group of 1st year students who are enrolled in the Army ROTC program. The ROTC living learning community creates camaraderie and team building among ROTC students. It connects ROTC students with other ROTC students, allowing them to room together, as they share a common goal.
- Students in the ROTC program have access to many different opportunities to enhance their abilities and prepare them for their future careers.
- Students in the ROTC LLC enroll in the Military Science 101 class in the fall, and the Military Science 102 class in the spring. They'll engage with their LLC Fellow, and benefit from programming tailored to their needs and interests.

### LT Eric Yates 5K Memorial Run
- Cadets and the community take part in the annual 5K memorial run.
- LT Yates, a 2008 WKU graduate, was killed in action Sept. 17, 2010, after an improvised explosive device detonated near his patrol in Afghanistan. LT Yates served in the Army’s 1st Battalion, 502nd Infantry Regiment, 2nd Brigade Combat Team, 101st Airborne Division from Fort Campbell.
- Also referenced: LT Yates Lounge Remodel

### German Armed Forces Proficiency Badge (GAFPB)
- The GAFPB - It’s one of only a few foreign awards that US Soldiers can wear. To earn the prestigious award you must complete 6 different challenges which include: Pistol Qualification, First Aid Course, Fitness Test, NBC MOPP Test, Foot March, 100 meter swim in military uniform. There are 3 levels of awards: Gold, Silver, or Bronze.
