# Military Science and Leadership

## World-class Leadership Training

The WKU Military Leadership major develops culturally aware leaders through interdisciplinary coursework and Army training. The program combines state-of-the-art training facilities with world-class education in leadership. Graduates are prepared for leadership roles, whether they pursue a commission in the U.S. Army or other fields, with opportunities open to veterans and non-commissioned officers.

## Scholarship Opportunities & Financial Support

WKU Army ROTC provides scholarships covering 100% of tuition and fees, with additional room and board options. Scholarships are available for two, three, or four years, and graduate-level scholarships cover 100% of tuition.

## WKU Living Learning Communities

The Army ROTC Living Learning Community consists of a diverse group of first-year students who are engaged in the Army ROTC program, and a variety of co-curricular activities. The living learning community will create camaraderie and team building among ROTC students, ensure each ROTC student is persistent in their academics and connect ROTC students with faculty/staff to track student success through mentorship opportunities.
