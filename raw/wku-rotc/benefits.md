# How can Army ROTC benefit me?

Army ROTC can help students succeed during college and afterwards: confidence, self-discipline, leadership skills and credentials, and opportunities for service as an officer on active duty or part-time while pursuing a civilian career. Also used to pay for a WKU education via scholarships.

After graduation, a commissioned officer can serve part-time in the Army National Guard or Army Reserves, or full-time in the Active Army. Marketing also cites corporate leadership/management skills, Army Nurse career path, and work with technical equipment.
