# Army Nursing - ROTC and WKU School of Nursing

ROTC nursing students combine military science electives and nurse summer training with the regular nursing program. Upon completion (and provided all prerequisites are met), they receive a commission as an officer in the Army Nurse Corps.

## Nurse Summer Training Program (NSTP)

Paid training program normally attended the summer after the first medical-surgical clinical, or the junior year. Clinical training in multiple hospital areas; assigned an Army Nurse preceptor; follows the preceptor duty schedule. Possible training areas listed: vital signs, patient-care planning, nutrition/feeding, range of motion and mobility, respiratory needs, medication administration, emergency procedures, IV therapy, and other techniques "not normally allowed in a civilian hospital."

## Continued education (after commissioning)

Specialty courses listed: Psychiatric/Mental Health, Pediatric, Perioperative, OB/GYN, Critical Care/ICU, Emergency/Trauma. After career status and eligibility, may apply for graduate programs such as Anesthesia Nursing or Health Care Administration, or graduate education at the school of choice.
