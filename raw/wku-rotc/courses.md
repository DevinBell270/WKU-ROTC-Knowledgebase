# Courses of Instruction

## Basic course

Four-semester block, normally freshman and sophomore years. Freshman year: two hours of instruction each week for two semesters. Sophomore year: three hours of instruction each week for two semesters. Emphasis: team and leadership development, hands-on equipment instruction, land navigation, leadership skills training.

Residence credit requires MIL 101, 102, 201, and 202. Alternate: MIL 210, described here as a 28-day camp at Fort Knox. Prior service / Guard / Reserve may receive advanced placement for part or all of the basic course. Basic course or equivalent is required for advanced course eligibility.

### Website course descriptions (titles differ from 2026–2027 catalog)

- **MIL 101 Mountaineering & Leadership (2 hours)** — Introduction to military mountaineering operations and leadership. Fundamentals of basic rappelling, belaying, and application of leadership in practical exercises. Field trips to off-campus locations may be required; transportation provided.
- **MIL 102 Developmental Skills (2 hours)** — Problem solving, critical thinking, leadership theory, basic first aid, survival skills, group interaction.
- **MIL 201 Basic Leadership (3 hours)** — Prerequisites MIL 101, 102 or PMS permission. Small unit leader prep: leadership techniques, doctrine, ethics, communications, tactical scenarios, land navigation, first aid, cultural awareness.
- **MIL 202 Team Building and Military Doctrine (3 hours)** — Prerequisites MIL 101, 102, 201 or PMS permission. Doctrine, tactics, values, ethics, officership, oral and written communication, U.S. Army from Vietnam to present.
- **MIL 210 Military Science Practicum (Basic Camp; 6 hours)** — In lieu of MS 101, 102, 201, and 202. Fort Knox, summer. Paid approximately $600. Must register before departure. Summer only.

## Advanced Course (MIL III and MIL IV)

Designed to commission officers for active duty and reserve. Successful completion earns a commission as a Second Lieutenant. Four semester-long courses plus a 33-day summer course. Additional military history course, normally junior or senior year, required prior to commissioning.

Must graduate Advanced Camp (AC) before commissioning. AC normally between junior and senior year. Paid approximately $600.00 while attending AC. Army furnishes travel, uniforms, quarters, medical care, and rations.

Advanced course cadets may be eligible for Airborne, Air Assault, Northern Warfare, and CTLT-style time with active units. Voluntary; high standards.

### Advanced course applicant requirements

- U.S. citizen or DA-approved alien category (approval before enrollment)
- At least 17 at enrollment and not reach 30 at commissioning (may be waived)
- Medically qualified per DA standards
- Completed basic course or Leader’s Training Course or equivalent; veterans with honorable discharges may qualify
- Minimum 2.0 GPA and at least 60 credit hours
- Selected by the PMS
- Written agreement to complete advanced course, attend AC, accept a commission in the Army Reserve, and serve a prescribed tour

Note on page: advanced courses may be taken even if not seeking a commission but majoring in Military Science.

### Website advanced descriptions

- **MIL 301 Military Leadership and Management (3 hours)** — PMS permission. Leadership/management, weapons intro, ethics and professionalism.
- **MIL 302 Military Leadership and Advanced Tactical Skills (4 hours)** — PMS permission. Small unit tactics and communications, map reading, troop leading procedures.
- **MIL 401 Professional Leadership Skills (3 hours)** — PMS permission. Leadership principles, organizational leadership, counseling, management, delegation and control. Links to https://www.wku.edu/rotc/documents/401.pdf
- **MIL 402 Role of the Army Officer (4 hours)** — PMS permission. Combat operations, logistics, administration, readiness, military justice, leadership/management, commissioning prep.
- **MIL 410 Military Science Practicum (6 hours; LDAC)** — PMS permission. Five-week summer field course. Successful completion required.
- **MIL 490 Military Leadership Seminar (1 hour)** — PMS permission. Transition to officer status; field experience on military installations required.

## Basic Camp (narrative)

Fully-paid five-week training camp at Fort Knox, KY, normally between sophomore and junior years. For students who have not taken Military Science I and II; medically qualified; transferring from a school without ROTC; junior/community college graduate entering an ROTC school with no prior AC/RC experience; graduate student starting a two-year program; high-school graduate entering a military junior college. Prior military service receives Basic Course credit and is not required to attend Basic Camp.

Students at Basic Camp may compete for a two-year Army ROTC scholarship.
