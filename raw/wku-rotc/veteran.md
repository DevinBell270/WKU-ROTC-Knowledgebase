# WKU and the Veteran

## ROTC credit for military service

Military experience may satisfy Basic Course credit (first two years). If credit is granted and the student is not on an Army ROTC scholarship, they can skip freshman/sophomore ROTC and enroll directly in the Advanced Course.

## Advanced Course / Advanced Camp

Advanced Course: leadership, organization, management, tactics, ethics and professionalism. Advanced Camp between junior and senior years; page describes it as six weeks, paid.

## Financial help

May compete for an in-college ROTC scholarship. May also collect any VA benefits normally entitled.

## Age at commissioning (this page is more specific than the catalog)

- Scholarship cadets must be under 31 on 31 December of the year they are to be commissioned. Described as set by law and non-waiverable.
- Non-scholarship cadets must not be 39 or older at projected commissioning.
- No waiver requirement for non-scholarship cadets through age 34 at projected commissioning.
- Brigade Commander is waiver authority for applicants who will be between 35 and 39 at projected commissioning.

## SMP

Selected students may participate in ROTC and enlist in ARNG or USAR if a vacancy exists. Paid at least Sergeant E-5 for Guard/Reserve service plus ROTC Advanced Course allowance. Serve as officer trainee performing 2LT-commensurate duties. After commissioning may stay with the unit or apply for active duty. For SMP in Kentucky, contact the Kentucky National Guard.

Prior active-duty enlisted time counts toward longevity pay and retirement if returning to active duty as an officer.
