# History of WKU ROTC

National Defense Act of 1916 authorized Army ROTC. WKU program is described as one of the oldest in Kentucky. Origin tied to Spring 1917 / U.S. entry into WWI. Male enrollment at Western Kentucky State Normal School then 264.

- Spring 1918: first campus military training via Student's Army Training Corps (S.A.T.C.).
- 1 October 1918: War Department authorized formal S.A.T.C. at Western; barracks erected.
- 28 January 1919: ROTC became a permanent part of the university.
- First temporary commander: 1LT Owen Ellsworth. First Professor of Military Science and Tactics (March 1919): LTC Wilford Twyman.
- First organized as Junior Division (two years basic only). Rifle team won National ROTC Rifle Match 1928–1933 (six consecutive years).
- September 1935: Senior Infantry Branch Oriented Division under MAJ John Robeson.
- 4 February 1937: Pershing Rifles charter — Company B, 3rd Regiment.
- May 1937: first Senior ROTC commissioning class.
- WWII: continued commissioning for European and Pacific theaters.
- Summer 1947: Air Force ROTC added; first Professor of Air Science MAJ William N. Pace, Jr. Separate AFROTC org in 1949. Terminated 1 July 1957 (enrollment, technical complexity, no engineering college). ~300 Air Force officers in 10 years.
- 16 January 1954: Scabbard and Blade Company C, 11th Regiment.
- 1954: Infantry Branch Program converted to General Military Science Program (commission into any branch).
- February 1962: academic minor in Military Science approved.
- ROTC Act of 1964 (ROTC Revitalization Act): two-year advanced course via Basic Camp; more scholarships; higher subsistence; clothing allowance; JROTC.
- 1964: Pershing Rifles B-3 sponsored Rebelettes (female auxiliary / drill). National champions 1967, 1973, 1975.
- 1964: new rifle range. NRA sponsor 1968; NCAA varsity rifle 1979.
- October 1967: Special Forces Company formed.
- September 1973: women authorized in ROTC academic program. First WKU female officer: 2LT Lynne F. Berry, MI, USAR, 14 May 1977.
- SY 1978–79: Early Commissioning Program authorized.
- May 1979: Simultaneous Membership Program authorized.
- April 1980: WKU cadre established UofL Army ROTC; UofL independent August 1982.
- August 1983: Pershing Rifles and Rebelettes retired; replaced by Red Knights.
- Spring 1996: WKU ROTC Alumni Association and first 7 Hall of Fame inductees.
- November 2003: Veterans Day ceremony at Guthrie Tower; Murray State partnership; Murray contracting began fall 2004.
- Spring 2005: MS100 classroom dedicated as Captain Charles F. Thomas IV Room of Honor.
