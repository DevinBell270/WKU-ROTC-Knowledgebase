# WKU Army ROTC Scholarship

WKU Army ROTC provides scholarships that cover 100% of tuition and fees, as well as a room and board scholarship that covers on-campus housing and 10 meals per week. Two-year graduate level scholarships cover 100% of tuition and fees.

**Email:** army.rotc@wku.edu

## What Army ROTC Scholarships offer

- 2, 3, or 4-year scholarships available (depending on graduation date)
- Pays ALL tuition costs and fees
- $1200 for books per year ($600 per semester)
- $420 per month ROTC stipend

## WKU Army ROTC Room and Meal Incentive

With a GPA of 3.0 or higher, attend class, lab, and physical training, you may be eligible for:

- Room Scholarship: covers the base rate of housing at WKU
- Meal Scholarship: 10 meals per week

The page states the total scholarship covers over $80,000.

## Minimum qualifications for an Army ROTC scholarship

- Be a U.S. citizen
- Be between the ages of 17 and 29
- Have a high school or college GPA of at least 2.5
- Take the ACT or SAT (no minimum score required)
- Meet physical and medical standards
- Agree to accept a commission and serve in the Army on Active Duty, or in a Reserve Component (U.S. Army Reserve or Army National Guard)
