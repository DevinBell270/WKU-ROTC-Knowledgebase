# Major in Military Leadership

Reference #733. Requires 36-42 hours; Bachelor of Science. 120 hours necessary for graduation. Remaining hours: approximately 44 hours general education and 34-40 hours from a required second major or minor. Students are encouraged to choose a second major related to intended career objectives.

## Admission

Program graduates are not required to seek commissions. Prospective officers, NCOs, veterans, and non-military students seeking leadership knowledge may be admitted. Priority to commission-seeking students. Admission is limited and based on qualifications. No restrictions regarding age, sex, race, or religious persuasions. (Age restriction in the commission-seeking list still applies for those seeking a commission.)

### Non-commission seeking

- U.S. citizen or DA-approved alien category
- Medically qualified per DA standards
- Minimum overall GPA 2.0
- Recommended by the PMS (Department Head)

### Commission seeking

- Same citizenship, medical, GPA 2.0, and PMS recommendation
- At least 17 at enrollment and not reach 30 at commissioning (may be waived)
- Written agreement to complete two-year advanced course, attend LDAC, accept a commission in the Army Reserve, and serve a prescribed tour

## Curriculum (department site)

1. General Education — 44 hours (see catalog / Colonnade).
2. Basic Course — 7-10 hours, one of:
   - MIL 101 Military Mountaineer/Leadership (2), MIL 102 Developmental Skills (2), MIL 201 Basic Leadership (3), MIL 202 Team Building/Military Doctrine (3); **or**
   - MIL 209 Introduction to Military Life (1) **and** MIL 210 Military Science Practicum LTC (6); **or**
   - Placement credit through military service.
3. Advanced Course — 17-20 hours: MIL 301 (3), MIL 302 (4), MIL 401 (3), MIL 402 (4), and MIL 410 LDAC (6) **or** LEAD 400 Practicum in Leadership (3).
4. Restricted electives — 3 hours: HIST 494 U.S. Military History.
5. Electives — 9 hours, two disciplines must be represented. Courses listed (each 3 hours): COMM 240, COMM 461, COMM 463; GEOG 462, 464, 465, 466, 467; HIST 426, 439; LEAD 200, 400; MGT 210, 419; PSY 355, 412; SOCL 260, 362, 375; PS 250, 260, 350, 357, 363, 365, 366, 367, 368, 457.
6. Second major or minor. Total 120 hours.
