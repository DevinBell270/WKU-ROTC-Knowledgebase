# 9/11 remembrance stair climb

WKU Army ROTC Facebook post (~Sep 12, 2026; caption references September 11).

**Caption (verbatim):**
"No day shall erase you from the memory of time."

This morning, September 11, the WKU Hilltopper Battalion Ranger Team climbed the stadium stairs in remembrance of all the lives lost 25 years ago. Every step they took honored the victims, the first responders, the service members, the family members and their families who are forever impacted by the events of September 11, 2001. Their courage, sacrifice, and memory will never be forgotten. Today, they climbed for those who no longer can. We remember, honor, and never forget.
