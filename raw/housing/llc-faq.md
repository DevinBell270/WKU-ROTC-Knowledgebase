# FAQ — Living Learning Communities

Selected answers from the Housing LLC FAQ. Contact: **llc@wku.edu**.

## Apply for one LLC

Students can apply for **1 (one)** Living Learning Community in the WKU Housing Portal after they have submitted their housing application in the Admissions Application Status Portal.

Steps: housing application → instructions from Housing & Residence Life → Microsoft Authenticator → login my.wku.edu → Housing Portal → Apply for Housing → correct academic term → complete application.

## Cost

There is **no additional cost** to participate in an LLC. Some LLCs require specific classes which may include course fees. LLC participants are assigned to the residence hall that houses their LLC. Rates: wku.edu/housing/halls/rates.

## Time commitment / events

Strongly encourage participation in LLC events as academic schedule permits. Time commitment can vary; expect a **minimum of 1–2 hours for a regular week**. Upon accepting an offer, students review an **LLC Learning Contract** with involvement expectations. The contract outlines any required program attendance.

## Honors + another LLC

Yes: Honors Living Learning Community, **or** an LLC more specific to the major. The student must live in the residence hall required of their LLC.

## Major change after applying

Email Admissions (admission@wku.edu / admissions@wku.edu) with WKU ID to change major. Also email llc@wku.edu, especially if the new major no longer aligns with the LLC applied for.

## Already have credit for the required LLC course

Email llc@wku.edu once applied. Admission into the LLC is at the discretion of the LLC Fellow.

## Roommate rules

All LLC students are **required to live with another person from their LLC**.

If accepted and the preferred roommate is on the waitlist for the same LLC, they would be placed together if the preferred roommate receives an offer.

To live with a non-LLC preferred roommate instead of the LLC: email llc@wku.edu and update living preference in the Housing Portal to “requested roommate” as top preference.

Once admitted, students can view other admitted LLC students in the Housing Portal. Roommate groups can be created if both are applying/accepted into the same LLC. LLC students can continue to search for a roommate after the **March 31 priority housing deadline**.

Do not rush to find an LLC roommate before accepting an LLC offer.

## Change LLC preference

Email llc@wku.edu. Once submitted, the LLC application cannot be changed in the Housing Portal without staff assistance.

## When admitted (dates on this capture)

Email with LLC decision. Apply prior to **February 4, 2026** → status **February 13**. Respond by **February 27, 2026**. After February 5: rolling. No response by deadline → space to waitlist.

These February 2026 dates are elapsed as of capture 2026-09-17.

## Waitlist and housing

Being on the LLC waitlist does **not** prevent a housing assignment. Assignment is based on the date applied. If an LLC space becomes available, Housing emails; joining the LLC updates the housing assignment.

## M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan

All first-year LLC students are required to attend M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan.

## Learning agreement

Complete the LLC Learning Agreement in the Housing Portal homepage: “complete your LLC Learning Contract,” then check the box after reviewing.

## After freshman year

WKU offers a **Second-Year LLC**. Interdisciplinary; open to students of all majors; designed for second-year students who participated in an LLC their first year.
