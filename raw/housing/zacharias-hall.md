# Zacharias Hall

Three-story suite-style residence hall built in 1992. Two rooms share a Jack and Jill bathroom. Houses **206 students**. South end of campus, surrounded by a large green space. Co-ed.

**Population line on page:** Available to First Year Students in a Living Learning Community.

Closes during Thanksgiving, Winter, and Spring breaks.

Address: **1755 Normal Street**, Bowling Green, KY 42101-6433. Front desk **(270) 936-0219**.

Amenities listed: wifi, ethernet, A/C, elevators, vending, ice, private suite-style bathrooms, laundry room with free laundry. Community: study room, TV lounge, kitchen on every floor, lobby, 24-hour security & information desk, mailboxes in lobby.

Page HTML meta description still says Zacharias “houses 206 upperclass students” — that meta line conflicts with the visible population line (first-year LLC). Use the visible page body, not the meta tag.

Rates are not printed on this page; it links to residence hall rates.
