# Residence Halls (LLC-relevant excerpt)

Living Learning Communities (general definition on this page): students with similar academic or social interests live together on a residence hall floor and participate in activities tailored to their specific majors or interests. Students must apply for an LLC on their housing application.

## Halls tagged Living Learning Communities (2026–2027 copy)

- **Minton Hall** — Co-Ed | Living Learning Communities. Center of campus, steps from Downing Student Union; near primary academic building for LLCs assigned here.
- **Meredith Hall** — Co-Ed | Living Learning Communities. First Year Village. “Meredith houses students in a Living Learning Community for the 2026-2027 academic year.”
- **Zacharias Hall** — Co-Ed | Living Learning Communities. First Year Village. “Zacharias houses students in a Living Learning Community for the 2026-2027 academic year.”
- **Southwest Hall** — Honors | Co-Ed | Second Year Living Learning Communities. Hotel-style; typically Honors College (sophomore+) and second-year LLC students.

This index does **not** say which LLC sits in which hall. For ROTC specifically see the ROTC LLC page (Zacharias) and the Fall 2025 assignments page (Meredith that year).
