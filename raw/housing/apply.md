# Apply for WKU Housing (LLC-relevant excerpt)

After admission to WKU, students submit a housing application through the Admissions Application Status Portal: https://apply.wku.edu/apply/status

## Housing application fields (as listed)

- Name, WKU ID, term, personal phone, personal email
- ADA accommodation needs
- Missing person contact
- Pre-existing medical conditions
- Top three living preferences, including **Living Learning Community** (also Honors housing, most affordable living, pod-style, requested roommate, single gender, suite or hotel style, university break/extended living)
- If under 18: legal parent or guardian contact
- Housing contract review, electronic signature
- **$75 housing application fee** (credit/debit)

If under 18, a form is sent to parent/guardian to complete. Checklist shows a checkmark when all required signatures are in.

## After housing application

Email from Housing & Residence Life with instructions to:

- Complete the Living Learning Community application
- Complete the roommate matching profile
- Select a roommate

LLC application and roommate profiles are in the **WKU Housing Portal via my.wku.edu**, after the housing application is processed. Students receive an email when the LLC/roommate portion is available.

Housing Portal path: my.wku.edu → Housing Portal → Apply for Housing → correct academic term → continue → complete application.

## Changes

Changes can be made through the Housing Portal through **March 31st**. Assignments are made in **date-received order from when the deposit is submitted**. Making changes does not affect date-received status.

## Roommates (general housing, not LLC-specific)

Application allows: add a specific person, search with matching, or random roommate. Requires a Roommate Screen Name, personal description, and matching questions.

For students selected in an LLC: “you will be asked to choose an LLC roommate in April.” Preferred roommate can be added in the roommate group section earlier.
