# Fall 2025 Housing Assignments (LLC location excerpt)

Dated Housing page about Fall 2025 assignments. Last modified 11/24/25 on the page footer.

Living Learning Community students were assigned to **Meredith, Zacharias, and Minton Halls**. Housing stated LLC students would see cost savings compared to Normal and Regents Halls.

## Which LLCs were in which hall (Fall 2025)

**Meredith Hall:** Agriculture; Creative Arts; Crime & Culture; Living PCAL; Media; **ROTC**; Stonewall Suites.

**Zacharias Hall:** Business; Earth, Environmental & Atmospheric Sciences (EEAS); Ogden Sciences; School of Engineering & Applied Sciences (SEAS).

**Minton Hall:** College of Health and Human Services (CHHS); Chinese Flagship; Education; Exercise Science; F1RST Gen; ISEC; Mahurin Honors College; Nursing; PSYCH.

## Hall type notes (Fall 2025)

Meredith and Zacharias: suite-style; two bedrooms share a bathroom; First Year Village, across the lawn from Normal and Regents. Housing rate printed on this page: **$3,395/semester** (stated as $665/semester savings vs Normal/Regents). Treat as a **Fall 2025** figure; do not use as a current rate without the live rates page.

Minton: community-style; center of campus near DSU.

Housing: “While the LLC locations may change, the high-quality programs and experiences will not.”
