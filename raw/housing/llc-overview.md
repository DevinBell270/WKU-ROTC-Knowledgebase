# WKU Living Learning Communities (overview)

WKU Housing & Residence Life. Contact: **llc@wku.edu**, **(270) 745-3577**.

## Definition

In a WKU Living Learning Community (LLC), students with similar academic interests live together on a residence hall floor and participate in activities tailored to their majors and areas of study.

LLCs are designed to help students connect socially, succeed academically, engage with WKU faculty, staff, and administrators, and chart their course at WKU.

## Published benefits / rules

- There is **no additional cost** for an LLC. Some LLCs require students to register for specific classes; individual LLC descriptions list additional requirements. Housing rates apply for the location of the LLC.
- Academic LLCs may include enrollment in **up to two linked courses** that count toward the major and/or General Education requirements.
- **Priority housing** — LLC students receive a housing assignment before other students.
- **Priority course registration** for LLC courses.
- Opportunity to form deeper connections with faculty and staff.
- Opportunity to connect with similar peers and build a support system.

## Outcome statistics (as cited on this page)

Attributed to “WKU Living Learning Communities Evaluation (2025)”:

- Students who participate in LLCs at WKU graduate at an **8.0% higher rate** than non-LLC peers.
- First-generation students in LLCs graduate at nearly double the rate of non-LLC peers (**36.8% vs. 19.0%**).
- Low-income LLC students see an **11.4 percentage point** increase in four-year graduation rates.

## How to apply (as published)

1. Submit a WKU Housing Application in the Admissions Application Status Portal.
2. After the housing application is processed (typically within 24 hours), the student receives an email directing them to the WKU Housing Portal to complete the LLC application and roommate matching profile.
3. Login to the WKU Housing Portal via my.wku.edu.
4. Select “Apply for Housing” from the navigation menu.
5. Select the appropriate academic term.
6. Review personal details and complete the application.

To change LLC after applying: email llc@wku.edu with WKU ID. Selecting a new LLC from the drop-down on the housing application **will not** update the LLC application.

All LLC applications are reviewed by the LLC Fellows. Space is limited and an offer is not guaranteed. Waitlists are available.

## Placement vs waitlist

**Placement offer:** accept or decline in the Housing Portal within the timeframe. To accept, confirm the LLC Learning Contract. Denying allows applying for a different LLC. Accepting assigns the student to the building/floor housing that LLC.

**Waitlist offer:** added if a placement becomes available. Student may deny the waitlist and apply for a different LLC.

## M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan

WKU’s transition program for new undergraduates. During M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan, LLC participants have special programming, meet their LLC faculty/staff fellow, and begin forming LLC bonds.

**All first-year Living Learning Community students are required to attend M.A.S.T.E.R. Plan.**
