# Military Science, Minor (420)

## Program Requirements (26-30 hours)

Select one of the following (6-10 hours):

- MIL 101, MIL 102, MIL 201, and MIL 202 — Introduction to the Army; Foundations of Army Leadership; Army Leadership and Ethics; Team Building and Army Doctrine
- MIL 210 — Military Science Practicum (Leader’s Training Course)
- Credit for the basic course through military service (i.e., completion of the Basic Course)

Then:

| Course | Title | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIL 301 | Army Training Management and Warfighting Functions | 3 |
| MIL 302 | Applied Tactical Army Leadership | 4 |
| MIL 401 | Professional Leadership Skills | 3 |
| MIL 402 | Role of the Army Officer | 4 |
| MIL 410 | Military Science Practicum (Leadership Development and Assessment Course) (Advanced Course) | 6 |
| **Total** | | **26-30** |

Those students who complete the advanced course may use military science as an academic minor. A minor in military science requires 26-30 hours depending upon the amount of advanced placement awarded the student.
