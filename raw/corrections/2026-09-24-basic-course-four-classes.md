# Correction: Basic Course before contracting

Program-staff statement from a meeting on 2026-09-24, relayed by Devin Bell.

Students can take the full two-year Basic Course before deciding to contract: four classes, MIL 101, MIL 102, MIL 201, and MIL 202 (freshman and sophomore years). Some recruiting copy has wrongly said students get only two classes before they have to commit.
