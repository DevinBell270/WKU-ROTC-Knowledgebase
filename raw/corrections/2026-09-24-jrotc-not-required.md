# Correction: JROTC not required

Program-staff statement from a meeting on 2026-09-24, relayed by Devin Bell.

Students often ask whether they must have done Junior ROTC (JROTC) in high school to join Army ROTC at WKU. The answer is no. JROTC experience in high school is not required to join Army ROTC at WKU.
