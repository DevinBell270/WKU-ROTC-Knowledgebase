# Veterans Day wreath laying (College Heights Herald)

11 November 2025, Guthrie Tower. Ceremony organized by WKU ROTC and Military Science and Leadership.

- **Department Chair Army Lt. Col. David Schnaak** quoted as department chair.
- **MAJ Chase Attwood / Atwood** (spelled both ways in the article) identified as Assistant Professor of Military Science; opened the program.
- Cadet Sam Humble gave the invocation.
- Kent Johnson, USMC veteran, director of Military Student Services.
- Retired Army MAJ Wade Tally, WKU ROTC 1974.
- History claim in article: military traditions date to 1917 Student Army Training Corps under the National Defense Act of 1916; permanent ROTC in early 1919; officers in every major conflict from WWII onward.
- Guthrie Tower dedicated 2002 by former President Gary Ransdell; funded by Lowell Guthrie in tribute to combat veterans; named after Robert “Bobby” Guthrie.
- Article cites WKU as No. 1 in Kentucky and No. 37 nationally on Military Times “Best for Vets.”
